# Glossary — Footprint's business terms

Plain-English meanings of words the business uses. Add to this whenever a new term comes up.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Flatplan | A page-by-page plan of a magazine issue showing where each article and advert goes. In Mag Manager: a colour-coded page grid plus a visual plan where adverts are placed onto pages (seen 27 October 2026). |
| Ad booking | An order from an advertiser for space in a specific edition and issue: advert size, price (set on each booking), salesperson, invoiced or not. |
| Edition | One of the 11 local-area versions of Forget Me Not, lettered A to K: Gosport, Stubbington & Lee-on-the-Solent, Portchester & Drayton, Fareham, Locks Heath and Whiteley, Warsash & Hamble, Hedge End, West End & Boorley Green, Chandler's Ford, Fair Oak & Boyatt Wood, Meon Valley. Around 50,000 homes a month in total (owner, 27 October 2026). |
| Issue | One month's magazine for one edition (e.g. Fareham November 2026), with its own page count, copy deadline and sales target. |
| Renewal (magazines) | An advertiser who booked a previous issue but not the current one. Mag Manager scores each by how recently they booked and whether they have future bookings, to prioritise calls. |
| Flipbook | The online page-turning version of an issue, published on Footprint's website. |
| Distributor | A person paid to hand-deliver Forget Me Not magazines on a GPS-tracked route. |
| Your Lead Kit (YLK) | Footprint's CRM and automation product for clients — a white-labelled GoHighLevel. Stays on GoHighLevel. |
| Quote to invoice | The core sales flow in Zoho Books: quote → sales order → (purchase orders to suppliers) → invoice. |
| Footprint's order flow | Owner, 26 October 2026: **Quote, proposal or advert → sales order (SO) → purchase orders (POs), design request (DesReq) and project.** The sales order is the hub everything after the sale hangs off. |
| Full sales flow | Owner, 26 October 2026 (the more detailed version): **Customer → New opportunity (shows in the pipeline) → sales order → Create project, Create design, Create PO.** |
| DesReq | Design request: a job for the design team (a design job in FootprintOS). |
| Web hosting plan | A recurring hosting service Footprint provides to a client, currently recorded in Zoho CRM. |
| FootprintAI transition | The programme turning the digital unit into FootprintAI and putting AI to work across the group (from 28 October 2026). Tracked in FootprintOS → Transition. |
| Week (transition) | A Wednesday meeting in the FootprintAI transition (Week 1 = Wed 28 Oct 2026, Week 8 = Wed 16 Dec, then After Christmas). Not the same as a FootprintOS build "wave". |
| Gate | A board decision point in the transition; nothing moves past it until signed off. |
