# Glossary — Footprint's business terms

Plain-English meanings of words the business uses. Add to this whenever a new term comes up.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Flatplan | A page-by-page plan of a magazine issue showing where each article and advert goes. _(To confirm with owner.)_ |
| Ad booking | An order from an advertiser for space in a specific issue. _(To confirm with owner.)_ |
| Edition | One of the 11 local-area versions of Forget Me Not (e.g. Fareham, Hedge End). Each goes to about 5,000 homes a month. |
| Distributor | A person paid to hand-deliver Forget Me Not magazines on a GPS-tracked route. |
| Your Lead Kit (YLK) | Footprint's CRM and automation product for clients — a white-labelled GoHighLevel. Stays on GoHighLevel. |
| Quote to invoice | The core sales flow in Zoho Books: quote → sales order → (purchase orders to suppliers) → invoice. |
| Web hosting plan | A recurring hosting service Footprint provides to a client, currently recorded in Zoho CRM. |
