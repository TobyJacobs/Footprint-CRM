# Wave 5 (Magazines): questions for the Mag Manager meeting

_Prepared 27 October 2026 for Toby and the director. Bring Mag Manager open (sign in in Claude's browser pane) so Claude can look at each screen as you answer. Nothing in Mag Manager will be changed._

## 1. The magazine itself
1. What are the names of all 11 Forget Me Not editions? Do any share pages or adverts?
2. How many homes does Forget Me Not reach now: 55,000 or 100,000+?
3. What's the monthly timetable for an issue: booking deadline, artwork deadline, print date, delivery date?

## 2. Selling adverts
4. Which advert sizes and positions do you sell (e.g. full page, half, quarter, covers)? What's the price of each?
5. Are there discounts for booking several months or several editions at once?
6. Does anyone get commission on advert sales? If so, how is it worked out?

## 3. Flatplans and artwork
7. Who builds the flatplan, and how: is it made in Mag Manager or somewhere else?
8. Where does advert artwork come from (the customer or our designers), and how do you track whether it's arrived and been approved?

## 4. Billing
9. How are advertisers invoiced: each month, up front, or by Direct Debit?
10. Do Mag Manager invoices go into Xero or Zoho today?

## 5. What to keep and what to drop
11. Which Mag Manager screens and reports do you use every month? Which do you never touch?
12. What annoys you about Mag Manager, and what do you wish it did?
13. Which month would be the safest switch-over (between two issues), and when does the Mag Manager contract renew?
